Executive overview

Business value and risk elimination.

The operational challenge

R&D organizations, industrial innovators, and commercial research institutes live and die by their intellectual property. The moment an engineer, scientist, or patent attorney inputs patent claims, chemical formulations, circuit schematics, or unreleased algorithm designs into a public AI prompt, those trade secrets can be compromised or ingested into foundational training corpora.

The ePrivacy-AI resolution

ePrivacy-AI provides a walled-garden research workbench. Ingest entire institutional patent repositories, non-public research papers, and technical whitepapers into dedicated vector databases that operate with no external internet connection.

Key business outcomes

Complete trade secret immunity

Zero risk of exposing prior art analysis, pre-grant patent specifications, or novel formulation data to third-party model maintainers.

Accelerated freedom-to-operate (FTO) studies

Synthesize and compare complex patent claims against novel internal inventions in hours instead of weeks of manual legal review.

Unbroken research continuity

Cross-index decades of internal laboratory reports, field data, and experimental notes to prevent redundant, expensive experiments.

High-impact use cases

Where teams put it to work.

1

Prior Art & Patent Landscape Mapping

Evaluates proposed patent claims against millions of global patent filings and academic publications to identify novelty risks, patent white spaces, and potential freedom-to-operate (FTO) barriers.

2

Laboratory Notebook & Pre-Publication Literature Ingestion

Ingests unreleased laboratory notes, spectroscopic data, and internal assay records using localized multi-modal OCR, enabling researchers to query experimental findings without cloud exposure.

3

Automated Patent Claim Chart Drafting

Generates detailed claim-to-product mapping charts for patent prosecution and infringement analysis, cross-referencing competitor product technical sheets against issued claims.

4

Multi-Disciplinary Technical Literature Synthesis

Synthesizes thousands of domain-specific papers across disparate repositories (e.g., ArXiv, IEEE, PubMed) to identify conflicting methodology findings, formulation parameters, or novel chemical mechanisms.

5

Grant Application & Scientific Proposal Structuring

Assists principal investigators in framing national science or defense grant proposals by aligning research goals with grant rubrics, past funded abstracts, and institutional budget constraints.

6

Licensing Agreement & Royalty Audit Copilot

Extracts multi-tiered royalty calculations, field-of-use restrictions, cross-licensing rights, and patent expiration dates across legacy institutional technology transfer portfolios.

For technical buyers

Technical architecture and security specifications.

Multi-modal & scientific layout parsing

Integrated parsing pipelines optimized for dense scientific layouts, mathematical expressions (LaTeX), tabular matrices, and multi-tier chemical/engineering schematics using specialized local OCR and table-transformer models.

Hybrid lexical & semantic retrieval

Combines BM25 sparse keyword search (vital for exact chemical names, patent numbers, and technical terms) with dense HNSW vector search to ensure zero retrieval loss on specialized vocabularies.

Compute-bound batch inference pipelines

Native API job queuing for heavy offline batch runs. Ingest thousands of patent filings overnight using worker queues feeding local multi-GPU clusters managed by Ray or Slurm.

Air-gapped licensing

The entire software stack operates with static, cryptographically signed offline license keys, allowing deployment in physical high-security research enclosures without external internet access.
