The core problem

Public AI creates productivity. It also creates liability.

When employees or researchers use consumer cloud AI tools, sensitive organizational records, patient inquiries, unpublished intellectual property, and proprietary codebase data are uploaded to external vendor servers. Traditional cloud platforms retain prompts, train on your inputs, or expose you to third-party data breaches.

The ePrivacy-AI difference

⬡

100% Boundary Containment

Compute, embeddings, and chat histories remain strictly inside your private cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP VPC) or on-prem bare metal.

⇄

Total Model Independence

Switch between self-hosted open-weights (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek) and private commercial endpoints (OpenAI, Anthropic via private tenant) with zero code rewrites.

☰

Verifiable Audit Trails

Every prompt, retrieval citation, and agent decision is logged to your internal SIEM system without third-party tracking.
