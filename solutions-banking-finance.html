Executive overview

Business value and risk elimination.

The operational challenge

Financial institutions operate under strict statutory guardrails governing customer data, algorithmic auditing, and market abuse prevention. Cloud-based AI APIs present unacceptable structural risks: potential exposure of account figures, transmission of trading algorithms, and lack of deterministic audit trails demanded by banking regulators.

The ePrivacy-AI resolution

ePrivacy-AI deploys as a sovereign, self-contained AI cluster inside the bank’s certified tier-3/4 on-premise data centers or private cloud VPC. It empowers investment teams, credit underwriters, and internal auditors to interrogate massive financial corpora without leaking market positions or customer PII.

Key business outcomes

Compressed credit & underwriting timelines

Extract financial metrics, debt covenants, and cash flow trends from multi-year audited statements in seconds.

Zero market information leakage

Research hedge strategies, merger valuations, and equity positions without signaling market intent to commercial AI providers.

Deterministic fraud & anomaly ingestion

Connect local models to high-velocity operational logs to detect regulatory non-compliance, sanctions breaches, or operational discrepancies.

High-impact use cases

Where teams put it to work.

1

Commercial Credit Underwriting & Covenant Auditing

Ingests audited balance sheets, cash flow models, and notes to accounts to generate comprehensive credit approval memos, calculate debt service coverage ratios (DSCR), and verify borrower covenant compliance.

2

Equity & Fixed-Income Research Extraction

Summarizes broker research, competitor investor decks, and quarterly earnings call transcripts into standardized financial models, extracting segment margins and management commentary instantly.

3

AML, KYC & Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Tracing

Analyzes complex, multi-layered corporate registry filings, trust structures, and trade records to identify hidden beneficial owners and cross-check them against global sanctions watchlists.

4

Wealth Management Advisory & Portfolio Reporting

Synthesizes multi-asset portfolio performance data into custom, client-ready quarterly wealth commentaries aligned with individual investor risk mandates and fiduciary compliance guidelines.

5

Regulatory Reporting Automation (FINRA/SEC/Basel Frameworks)

Pre-populates complex statutory filing documentation (10-K, 10-Q, liquidity coverage ratio reports) by pulling verified numbers directly from general ledgers with complete line-item provenance.

6

Trade Finance Document Discrepancy Checking

Scans bills of lading, letters of credit (LC), insurance certificates, and commercial invoices to detect discrepancies with UCP 600 rules prior to trade settlement.

For technical buyers

Technical architecture and security specifications.

PII & cardholder masking pipeline

Streaming, client-side token sanitization layer. Account numbers, Social Security/National IDs, and contact credentials are dynamically redacted using deterministic regular expressions and localized Named Entity Recognition (NER) models before text enters vector embedding pipelines.

Database & ledger connectivity

Read-only database proxies with parameter-enforced query templates (preventing arbitrary SQL injection) allowing agents to retrieve balance information securely.

Multi-tier model ensembles

Integrates smaller, deterministic specialized models (such as tabular-specific language models) for structured financial balances, combined with 70B+ open-weight LLMs (via vLLM) for unstructured qualitative narrative analysis.

Cryptographic non-repudiation

Every inference run produces a cryptographically signed provenance record detailing the exact model checkpoint, weights hash, temperature, and system prompt used for execution.
