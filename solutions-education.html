Executive overview

Business value and risk elimination.

The operational challenge

Universities and educational institutions face dual challenges: students are using external, ungrounded commercial AI tools that bypass academic integrity and encourage passive plagiarism, while institutional leaders face soaring per-student software licensing fees that make campus-wide AI access financially unsustainable. Furthermore, feeding student work into external LLMs violates FERPA and institutional data custody mandates.

The ePrivacy-AI resolution

ePrivacy-AI installs inside the university’s private cloud or campus data center, connecting directly into your Learning Management System (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, D2L) via LTI 1.3. It empowers professors to deploy custom, course-grounded Socratic tutors that guide student thinking rather than simply spitting out answers, while guaranteeing that student prompts and research papers remain strictly within the university’s boundary.

Key business outcomes

Predictable campus-wide financials

Fixed infrastructure and platform licensing replace unpredictable per-student seat fees, allowing the institution to offer equal AI access to all students, faculty, and administrative staff.

Pedagogically grounded Socratic tutoring

Configure agents that follow pedagogical rubrics, asking guiding questions, prompting critical thinking, and referencing assigned course textbooks without hallucinating answers.

Absolute student privacy & FERPA compliance

Zero student data is transmitted to external model vendors or used to train public models.

Where teams put it to work.

Course-Grounded Socratic Learning Copilots

Provides students with 24/7 personal tutors grounded strictly in designated course syllabi, lecture slides, and assigned textbooks, prompting critical reasoning rather than supplying unearned direct answers.

Instructional Design & Curriculum Development Assistants

Empowers professors to design weekly course modules, generate balanced assessment pools (multiple-choice, scenario-based essays), and build standards-aligned grading rubrics in minutes.

Student Retention & Early Intervention Advising

Ingests LMS engagement metrics (attendance, assignment submission latency, quiz trends) to alert academic advisors to struggling students and draft personalized outreach recommendations.

Admissions, Financial Aid & Registrar Self-Service

Academic Integrity & Plagiarism Diagnostic Analysis

Evaluates student draft essays against course materials to check attribution accuracy, identify misquoted sources, and encourage proper citation standards before final grading.

For technical buyers

Technical architecture and security specifications.

LTI 1.3 Advantage interoperability

Native support for 1EdTech LTI 1.3 standards, including Assignment and Grade Services (AGS) for automated rubric evaluation, Names and Role Provisioning Services (NRPS) for roster syncing, and Deep Linking for seamless embedding within course modules.

Academic integrity & citation guardrails

Agent prompts configured with strict systemic negative constraints: models are instructed to refuse direct essay-writing tasks and instead operate in continuous Socratic dialogue, citing page and paragraph references directly from course materials.

Multi-tenant campus isolation

Departmental, faculty, and course-level workspace segregation. The School of Medicine, School of Law, and College of Engineering maintain separate vector indexes, model access rules, and administrative permissions within a unified platform deployment.

Low-cost commodity hardware execution

Tuned to run quantized open-weights (such as Llama 3 and Mistral 8B/70B) on cost-effective campus GPU clusters or affordable cloud compute, providing optimal performance per dollar spent.
