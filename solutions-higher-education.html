Use cases

Three ways campuses use it

Curriculum-Grounded Socratic Tutoring

ProblemGeneric AI tools write students' essays for them, encouraging academic dishonesty and returning inaccurate textbook facts.
FunctionNative LTI 1.3 modules connect to Canvas, Moodle, and Blackboard. Agents guide students step by step with Socratic prompts, pulling facts only from verified course materials.
OutcomeDeeper comprehension, zero essay ghostwriting, and complete LMS integration.

Secure Scientific & Grant Research

ProblemResearchers cannot use commercial AI for patent-pending discoveries, military research grants, or sensitive human subject surveys.
FunctionePrivacy-AI deploys directly onto university HPC clusters. High-parameter open models run isolated on internal GPUs with high-speed scratch storage.
OutcomeHigh-tier computational research with zero intellectual property leakage.

Automated Registrar & Financial Aid Operations

ProblemStudents overwhelm helpdesks with repetitive financial aid and enrollment questions, leading to multi-day backlogs and dropouts.
FunctionDeterministic agents ingest the university policy database and give definitive answers with citations, escalating edge cases into existing ticketing systems.
OutcomeRoutine administrative inquiries are answered instantly, and staff time goes to the cases that need people.
  • Automated grading assistance with rubrics
  • Grant proposal structuring
  • Multi-paper literature extraction
  • Automated syllabus ingestion
For technical buyers

What your security team will ask about.

LMS connectivity

Full LTI 1.3 (Advantage) compliance: Deep Linking, Assignment and Grade Services (AGS), and Names and Role Provisioning Services (NRPS). Canvas, Moodle and Blackboard.

Student privacy

Chat logs and student query patterns are stored only in the university's managed database. No prompt history is sent to model vendors for reinforcement learning. Zero external telemetry.

Research compute

Runs on campus HPC clusters with local GPUs. Grant data is never exported.

Compliance alignment

FERPA: student records, chat submissions, grades, and identities never leave the institution's private tenancy. Zero model training on student inputs.
