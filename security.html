Compliance is built in, not added on.

FERPA

Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act

Student records, chat submissions, grades, and identities never leave the institution's private tenancy. Zero model training on student inputs.

HIPAA

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

Configurable for Business Associate Agreement (BAA) alignment, or 100% self-hosted on-prem where a third-party BAA is unnecessary because data never touches external infrastructure.

GDPR & sovereign data

Data residency & right to erasure

Runs inside local data centers without foreign data transit. Localized database pruning on request.

SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001

Alignment

Granular role-based controls, immutable audit logging, and automated vulnerability scanning make the platform auditable by third-party assurance teams.

Security engineering specifications for CISOs & SecOps
Zero egress architecture

No licensing callbacks over the public internet. Licensing is validated with cryptographically signed offline certificates.

Encryption

AES-256 across vector stores, relational databases and volume mounts with AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault or HashiCorp Vault. Mandatory TLS 1.3 for all service-to-service traffic and user ingress.

Testing & scanning

Semi-annual third-party gray-box penetration testing. Base container images continuously scanned for CVEs (Debian and Alpine minimal).

Memory & in-flight isolation

RAG contexts and model caches are ephemeral. After an agentic run, transient context is flushed from GPU VRAM to prevent cross-tenant or cross-session residue.
