How self-hosted and on-premise deployment helps

All sectors

Because ePrivacy-AI runs in your own environment, prompts, documents and logs never reach us or a model vendor. There is no third-party processor to assess, no cross-border transfer to justify, you hold the encryption keys, and the audit trail stays in your own systems. That removes the vendor-risk, data-residency and transfer questions most regulations ask first, and leaves your own controls as the evidence of compliance.

Enterprise & Business

Employee prompts, supplier contracts and HR data stay inside your boundary, so AI use does not add a new third-party processor or a cross-border transfer. Your own logs support AI governance, risk assessments and human-oversight records.

Legal & Compliance

Client documents and work product never reach a vendor, which protects privilege and confidentiality. Matter-level data can be purged, and your own logs support the provenance and defensibility of AI-assisted review.

Banking & Finance

Account data, card numbers and trading information stay in your data center or private cloud, inside your own PCI and resilience controls. Immutable logs and signed run records support record-keeping and model validation, and there is no extra vendor to add to your third-party risk register.

IP & Research

Patent drafts, formulations and lab data stay on your hardware, so nothing is disclosed to a vendor that could weaken trade secret status. Local processing also keeps export-controlled technical data inside the jurisdiction and people you control.

Healthcare & Biomedical

Patient data is processed inside your network, so no outside processor handles it. De-identification runs before inference, and audit trails and memory flushes after each run reduce breach risk and support research data rules.

Public Sector & Defence

Everything runs on hardware you own, including disconnected networks, so sovereignty, residency and access-restriction rules are met by design. Offline licensing and signed model packages (Enterprise tier) fit controlled and classified environments.

Education & eLearning

Student prompts and records stay inside your own tenancy, so no vendor sees education records or children's data. You control retention and access, which makes consent, parental-rights and records requests easier to answer.

Security Engineering Specifications

For CISOs and SecOps teams.

Zero egress architecture

No licensing callbacks over the public internet. Licensing is validated with cryptographically signed offline certificates.

Encryption

AES-256 across vector stores, relational databases and volume mounts with AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault or HashiCorp Vault. Mandatory TLS 1.3 for all service-to-service traffic and user ingress.

Testing & scanning

Semi-annual third-party gray-box penetration testing. Base container images continuously scanned for CVEs (Debian and Alpine minimal).

Memory & in-flight isolation

RAG contexts and model caches are ephemeral. After an agentic run, transient context is flushed from GPU VRAM to prevent cross-tenant or cross-session residue.
