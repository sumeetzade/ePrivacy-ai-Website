For technical buyers

What your security team will ask about.

HIPAA alignment

Operates within HIPAA-compliant physical or VPC infrastructure. Configurable for Business Associate Agreement (BAA) alignment, or 100% self-hosted on-prem where a third-party BAA is unnecessary because data never touches external infrastructure.

PII / PHI sanitization

Client-side de-identification layers strip patient identifiers before inputs reach local inference endpoints.

Encryption

AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, with customer-managed KMS keys (AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault or HashiCorp Vault).

Deployment

Private cloud VPC, on-prem bare metal, or fully air-gapped. Outbound connectivity can be severed without interrupting agents or retrieval.
