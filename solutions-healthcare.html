Executive overview

Business value and risk elimination.

The operational challenge

Healthcare providers and biomedical companies handle Protected Health Information (PHI) under rigid statutory oversight (HIPAA, HITECH, GDPR). While generative AI holds transformative potential for reducing clinical documentation burden and synthesizing research protocols, cloud-based models introduce grave liabilities regarding patient identity breaches and unauthorized data aggregation.

The ePrivacy-AI resolution

ePrivacy-AI operates directly within the secure perimeter of your hospital network, research institution, or dedicated private cloud tenancy. Patient records, clinical notes, and clinical trial datasets remain within your direct physical and cryptographic custody, ensuring zero regulatory exposure under HIPAA.

Key business outcomes

Eliminated PHI data breach exposure

Ensure complete data localization where clinical notes, treatment histories, and genetic data never leave institutional boundary controls.

Reclaimed clinical administrative hours

Streamline the synthesis of unstructured discharge summaries, physician shift notes, and clinical trial documentation.

Accelerated protocol discovery

Give biomedical researchers instantaneous, grounded natural language access to internal medical libraries, institutional formularies, and clinical study data.

High-impact use cases

Where teams put it to work.

1

Institutional Formulary & Clinical Protocol Copilot

Provides hospital clinicians with instant, citation-backed guidance on drug interaction warnings, formulary alternatives, and departmental escalation procedures grounded exclusively on hospital guidelines.

2

Clinical Trial Cohort Identification & Protocol Matching

Ingests unstructured electronic health records (EHR summaries) and cross-checks them against dense clinical trial inclusion and exclusion criteria, identifying eligible trial candidates without exposing PHI.

3

Medical Record Summarization for Continuity of Care

Ingests longitudinal, multi-facility medical histories, pathology reports, and discharge notes to generate concise, chronological clinical handoff summaries for intake physicians.

4

Adverse Drug Event (ADE) & Pharmacovigilance Surveillance

Scans clinical encounter notes, internal patient safety reports, and post-marketing surveillance feeds to flag emerging drug-related adverse events and prepare regulatory safety reports.

5

Radiology & Pathology Narrative Report Structuring

Converts dense, unstructured free-text diagnostic dictations into standardized structured formats (e.g., BI-RADS, TNM staging) aligned with clinical registry reporting guidelines.

6

Prior Authorization & Insurance Appeal Generation

Automatically extracts relevant clinical justifications and diagnostic test histories from patient charts to draft evidence-backed prior authorization requests and denial appeals.

For technical buyers

Technical architecture and security specifications.

Automated client-side de-identification

Built-in de-identification engine (Safe Harbor method) automatically recognizes and masks all 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers (names, dates, geographic data, MRNs) prior to processing within inference loops.

Integration with healthcare standards

Architectural compatibility with HL7 and FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) data pipelines via secure API mediation gateways.

Isolated vector partitioning

Multi-tenant clinical isolation ensuring that individual department data (e.g., Oncology, Pediatrics, Pathology) can be compartmentalized using encrypted vector indices and zero cross-department data leakage.

VRAM sanitization & session ephemerality

Automated GPU memory flushes ensuring that intermediate context tensors and transient patient states are wiped from GPU memory immediately upon completion of inference.
