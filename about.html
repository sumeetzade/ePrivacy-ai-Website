About us

ePrivacy-AI is built by Enlink Managed Services Pvt Ltd.

Enlink is a technology services company. It works in training, technology-driven optimization and digital-media business growth. It was founded by the promoters of Jaysynth, an industrial colors company listed on the BSE.

Our story

How Enlink started and what it does today.

2000

Enlink began as a technical services and solutions company. Its first customer was Webhelp.com, for which it ran human-assisted search services across several search platforms, including Yahoo, MSN, Go.com, AltaVista and Google.

Early years

Over the next few years Enlink signed MSN, Hotmail, Gateway Computers, AOL and Norelco. The work covered technical support, customer service, and billing and refund management.

2004–2005

Enlink set up a joint venture in the US and acquired Lester Inc. This gave it a higher-value outsourcing niche serving the publishing, B2B media and fundraising sectors.

The next two decades

Enlink expanded its technology services and worked with leading internet businesses, including Register.com (now part of Web.com), and with many small and medium businesses.

Today

Enlink’s portfolio covers IT training, cloud and managed IT services, digital marketing and staff augmentation.
