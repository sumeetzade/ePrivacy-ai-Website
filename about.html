About us

A solution by Enlink Managed Services, built on two decades of technology services.

ePrivacy-AI is developed by Enlink Managed Services Pvt Ltd, a technology services company focused on training, technology-driven optimization, and digital-media business growth. Enlink was founded by the promoters of Jaysynth, an industrial colors company listed on the BSE.

Our history

From a technical services start-up to a managed IT partner.

  1. 2000

    A technical services company is founded

    Enlink starts as a technical services and solutions company. Its first customer is Webhelp.com, for assisted search across multiple search platforms.

  2. Early 2000s

    Support for global internet brands

    Clients include MSN, Hotmail, Gateway Computers, AOL, and Norelco, for technical support, customer service, and billing and refund management.

  3. 2004–2005

    US joint venture and acquisition

    Enlink sets up a US joint venture and acquires Lester Inc, entering niche outsourcing for the publishing, B2B media, and fund-raising sectors.

  4. Today

    Training, managed IT, and digital growth

    After two decades serving internet businesses such as Register.com and many small and medium businesses, Enlink offers IT training, cloud and managed IT services, digital marketing, and staff augmentation.
