Bring Your Own Compute (BYOC)

Software license from us, compute from you.

Traditional commercial AI SaaS sells you software and computing power bundled together with markups, charging you every time an employee asks a question. ePrivacy-AI works like enterprise database software.

You license the software

ePrivacy-AI provides the workspace, agent engine, document indexing, and privacy controls at a fixed annual fee.

You supply the compute

You run it on your own server in your office, or inside your private AWS, Azure or GCP account. You pay your hardware vendor or cloud provider directly at raw cost, with no markup from us.

Technical buyer FAQ

Can we upgrade tiers as our team grows?

Yes. Upgrades between Starter, Standard, Pro, and Enterprise take effect immediately. You only pay the pro-rated difference on your platform license, and your data and agents remain intact.

How is licensing validated in air-gapped environments?

Enterprise editions validate licensing with an offline, cryptographically signed license file issued to your hardware UUID or cluster ID. No external outbound pings or internet connections are ever required.

What hardware do we need for the Starter or Standard tier?

For 5 to 20 users, a modern workstation with a single NVIDIA GPU (e.g., RTX 4090, RTX 6000 Ada, or L4) or a single cloud VM (such as AWS g5.xlarge) running a 4-bit or 8-bit quantized model (e.g., Llama 3 70B or Qwen 2.5 32B) provides sub-second latency for typical office concurrency.

Can Starter or Standard users connect to commercial APIs?

Yes. All tiers are model-agnostic. If you choose not to run local GPU hardware, you can run the ePrivacy-AI web workspace and RAG pipeline on a standard low-cost CPU server and connect securely to your private Azure OpenAI or AWS Bedrock endpoints with zero data retention.
