Executive overview

Business value and risk elimination.

The operational challenge

Legal and compliance teams work with the highest-risk data in any enterprise: unannounced M&A transactions, trade secrets, litigation strategy, and material non-public information (MNPI). Standard cloud-hosted LLMs violate non-disclosure agreements, threaten attorney-client privilege, and introduce exposure to discovery subpoenas directed at third-party AI vendors.

The ePrivacy-AI resolution

ePrivacy-AI establishes a non-egress computing enclave for legal workflows. Your documents, queries, and generated legal work product never leave your infrastructure. The platform does not log data externally, retains zero operational memory after session termination, and guarantees that models never train on client matters.

Key business outcomes

Preservation of privilege & confidentiality

Retain ironclad attorney-client privilege and strict adherence to professional conduct mandates regarding non-disclosure.

Accelerated transaction velocity

Reduce M&A due diligence and vendor contract review cycles from weeks to hours by processing thousands of clauses concurrently.

Mitigated regulatory exposure

Audit corporate behavior and documentation against changing regulatory regimes (GDPR, CCPA, EU AI Act, statutory filing mandates) with traceable citations.

High-impact use cases

Where teams put it to work.

1

High-Volume Due Diligence & Data Room Extraction

Scans thousands of commercial leases, supplier contracts, and corporate debt agreements during M&A cycles to extract indemnification ceilings, assignment triggers, and change-of-control covenants.

2

Automated Redlining & Playbook Discrepancy Analysis

Performs clause-by-clause comparisons between counterparty markups and the organization's institutional negotiation playbooks, automatically inserting approved fallback clauses and generating risk rationale notes.

3

Statutory & Regulatory Change Mapping

Continuously indexes evolving statutes, regulatory bulletins (SEC, FTC, EU AI Act, ESG mandates), and case law, cross-referencing them against current corporate policies to highlight non-compliance gaps.

4

Litigation Discovery & Deposition Transcript Synthesis

Ingests terabytes of internal emails, chat exports, and deposition transcripts under attorney-client privilege boundaries, enabling legal teams to build chronological fact patterns and test cross-examination lines.

5

Multi-Jurisdiction Real Estate Title & Conveyancing Review

Extracts chain-of-title histories, encumbrances, easements, and sub-registrar registration records from scanned historical land records, spotting partition defects and title clouds.

6

Whistleblower & Internal Investigation Fact-Extraction

Analyzes confidential internal grievance reports and associated communications under an ephemeral, air-gapped sandbox, mapping witness statements without risking leaks or metadata exfiltration.

For technical buyers

Technical architecture and security specifications.

Zero-trace ephemeral sandboxes

Support for ephemeral compute sessions. Memory allocations and vector partitions associated with a specific matter or data room can be programmatically purged with cryptographic wipe verification upon matter closure.

Deterministic line-level citation

The Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engine enforces strict source-attribution tokens. Generated text links directly to the specific page, paragraph, and line coordinates of the ingested PDF/Word file to eliminate unchecked hallucination.

Encryption standards

End-to-end envelope encryption. Data at rest is encrypted via AES-256 utilizing external Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) or enterprise Key Management Services (AWS KMS, HashiCorp Vault). In-transit data is enforced via TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy.

Auditability & traceability

Immutable logging of user identity, query timestamp, model version, and exact context windows accessed, exportable to enterprise SIEM platforms (Splunk, Datadog) for regulatory audits.
